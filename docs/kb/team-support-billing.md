# Team, Support, Billing, Alerts & Events

## Team (admin only)

### How do I manage my company's users?

Open the **Team** tab (visible to the company admin only). There you can **Add a profile**, **Reset** a member's password, and **Remove** a member — all for your own company.

### How do I add a profile?

In Team, type the associate's name and click **Add profile**. The app generates their login and a one-time password to hand over; they set their own password on first login.

### Do profiles created by the super admin show up for the admin?

Yes. The super admin's company view and the admin's Team tab read the same member list, and updates sync live. A profile created by either side appears for both.

## Support

### How do I raise a support case?

Click **Create case** in the sidebar, choose a category and severity, add a title and description (and optional screenshots), and submit. You and the support team can then message back and forth on the case, and you'll be notified of replies.

### Where do I see my cases?

The Create case button opens the support center listing all your company's cases with their status (pending, in progress, resolved). Click a case to open the conversation.

## Billing & Plans (admin only)

### What plans are there?

- **Starter** — up to 30 drivers and 25 own warehouses.
- **Pro** — up to 70 drivers and 50 warehouses.
- **Enterprise** — unlimited drivers and warehouses.

Global (shared) warehouses don't count toward the warehouse limit.

### How do I see or change my subscription?

Open the **Billing** tab (admin only) to view your plan, status, and invoices. New companies start on a **7-day trial**; the plan itself is set by the platform operator.

### What happens when the trial ends?

When a trial expires (or a subscription is suspended/cancelled) the company is locked out until it's reactivated. The super admin can extend a trial.

## Alerts & Events

### What is the Alerts tab?

Alerts surfaces operational items that need attention (e.g., unassigned work or issues). The sidebar shows a badge with the current alert count.

### What is the Events tab?

Events is an activity log of actions taken in the company (who did what, when). It is **admin only**.
