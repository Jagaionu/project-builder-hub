# Drivers

The **Drivers** tab is your driver roster. Open it to add, edit, and remove drivers and to see their hours and compliance.

## How do I add a new driver?

In the **Drivers** tab, use the add-driver form (top of the page): enter the driver's **name**, optional **phone**, optional **home warehouse**, and whether **return to base** is required, then save. The new driver appears in the roster.

## How do I edit a driver?

Find the driver in the roster, open their edit form, change the details (name, phone, home warehouse, return-to-base), and save. The edit panel also has a **Schedule** section for setting the driver's weekly **shift pattern** (which days and hours they're on shift).

## How do I remove a driver?

Open the driver and use the delete/remove action. Removing a driver deletes them from the roster.

## How does a driver log in to the driver app?

Drivers connect via a pairing/login code or the device profile, depending on how the company is set up. A driver's live position then appears on the Live Map once their app is online.

## Why is a driver shown as OFF_SHIFT today?

A driver is OFF_SHIFT when they're outside their working hours, on a scheduled day off, or on holiday for that day. Off-shift drivers don't appear as active on the Live Map. Check the driver's shift schedule/holidays if you expected them to be working.

## What do the driver statuses mean?

- **OFF_SHIFT** — not working right now.
- **On shift / active** — working and available.
- **On route** — currently running an assigned route.

## How do I filter or find a driver?

Use the filters in the Drivers tab header, and the search box on the Live Map Fleet panel to find a driver by name.
