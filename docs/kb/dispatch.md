# Dispatch

The **Dispatch** tab is the dispatch board. It lists routes (VRIDs) in a queue with their status, and a detail panel for the selected route.

## What do the route statuses mean?

- **Pending** — created but not yet started/assigned.
- **Scheduled** — planned with a driver and start time.
- **In progress** — currently being driven.
- **Completed** — finished.
- **Cancelled** — cancelled.

The counters at the top of the page show how many routes are in each status.

## How do I create a route?

Click **Create route** on the Dispatch page, fill in the origin, destination, stops, timing, and (optionally) a driver, then save. The route appears in the queue as a VRID.

## How do I assign a driver to a route?

Open the route in the detail panel and choose a driver, or use the AI assistant ("assign driver"). You can't change the driver on a **completed** route.

## Why can't I change the driver on a completed route?

A completed route is finished and locked for record accuracy, so its assigned driver can't be changed.

## How do I find a route?

Use the "Filter by reference, driver, status…" search box at the top of the Dispatch page.

## What is "Audit Plan"?

Audit Plan reviews the current plan for issues (e.g., compliance or timing problems) before you commit to it.
