# Live Map

The **Live Map** (Live Operations) shows real-time fleet visibility: driver positions on the map and a **Fleet** list on the right.

## What do Connected / Active / On route mean?

- **Connected** — the driver's app is online and reporting location.
- **Active** — the driver is on shift and working.
- **On route** — the driver is currently running an assigned route.

The counters at the bottom-left of the map show how many drivers are in each state.

## Why does the Fleet show 0 drivers?

Either the company has no drivers yet, or no drivers are currently on shift / connected. Off-shift and holiday drivers are not listed as active.

## How do I find a driver on the map?

Use the search box next to the "Fleet / N drivers" header to filter the list by name, then click a driver to view their details.

## How do I see a single route on the map?

From a route's detail panel, use **View on map** to focus the map on that VRID's driver and stops.

## What are the orange numbered circles?

Those are clusters — groups of drivers/markers in the same area. The number is how many are clustered there; zoom in to separate them.
