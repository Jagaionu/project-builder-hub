# Shifts & Holidays

## How are driver shifts set?

Each driver has a weekly shift schedule that determines when they're on shift. Drivers outside their shift hours show as OFF_SHIFT and aren't treated as active.

## How do holidays work?

A dispatcher can mark a driver as on holiday for given days. A holiday day immediately forces the driver OFF_SHIFT for that day (live and after refresh), and the driver is dropped from the live map for that day.

## Can a driver set their own holiday for today?

No — drivers are blocked from setting a holiday for the current day; holidays are planned ahead. A dispatcher manages holidays.

## Why is a driver OFF_SHIFT even though they should be working?

Common reasons: it's outside their scheduled shift hours, it's a scheduled day off, or they've been marked on holiday for that day. Check the driver's shift calendar and holiday entries.

## Where do I see a driver's schedule?

On the Drivers tab, open the driver to view their shift calendar and status for the week.
