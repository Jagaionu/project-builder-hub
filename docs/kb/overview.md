# The Prime Route — Overview

The Prime Route is a UK logistics dispatch and driver-tracking platform. Each company (tenant) manages its own drivers, warehouses, routes, and live operations. Data is isolated per company.

## What are the main sections (tabs)?

- **Live Map** — real-time map of where drivers are, with a Fleet list on the right.
- **Dispatch** — the dispatch board: create and manage routes (VRIDs), assign drivers, track status.
- **Drivers** — the driver roster: add, edit, and remove drivers; see hours and compliance.
- **Warehouses** — the company's warehouses (locations); add and edit them.
- **Alerts** — operational alerts that need attention.
- **Events** — an activity log of what happened in the company (admin only).
- **Billing** — the company's subscription, plan, and invoices (admin only).
- **Team** — create and manage user profiles for the company (admin only).

## Who uses it?

- **Super admin** — the platform operator. Creates companies, generates the company admin login, and manages everything across all companies from the Admin console.
- **Company admin** — runs one company. Sees every tab, plus the admin-only Team, Events, and Billing tabs.
- **Member** — a regular company user (e.g., a dispatcher or associate). Sees Live Map, Dispatch, Drivers, Warehouses, Alerts, and the AI assistant — but not Events, Billing, or Team.

## How do I get help inside the app?

- Use the **AI Assistant** (the sparkle button in the sidebar) to ask questions about the app.
- Use **Create case** (in the sidebar) to raise a support ticket to the platform team.
